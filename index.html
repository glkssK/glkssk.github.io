Biography

I am an incoming jointly trained Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Institute of Technology and Qiyuan Laboratory, Tsinghua University, starting in 2027. I will be co-supervised by Prof. Ying Fu at Beijing Institute of Technology and Prof. Junliang Xing from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University.

Before joining the Ph.D. program, I spent time at Tsinghua University’s Institute for AI Industry Research (AIR) and Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School (SIGS), where I worked on generative modeling for human motion and video, as well as efficient inference for large generative models.

I am broadly interested in building generative world models for intelligent agents that can understand, predict, and interact with the physical world.

Research

My research interests span world models, embodied intelligence, latent-action learning, video generation, and multi-agent interaction and decision-making.

First authorSubmitted to AAAI 2027Mar – Jul 2026

MARC-Flow: Mask-Adaptive Residual Coordinates for Text-Conditioned Motion Completion

Zexuan Wang, Zeyu Ling, Xitian Huang, Yujie Hou, Yiwei Sheng

Separates keyframe-predictable structure from text-dependent residual motion, then learns completion in mask-adaptive residual coordinates with Flow Matching.

Flow MatchingMotion completionSparse keyframes
Method & contribution

Method

A frozen text-free prior estimates the predictable structure and base variation. A lightweight mask-adaptive scale adapter normalizes residual motion across time, body parts, and keyframe layouts; reconstruction restores the observed keyframes exactly.

Contribution

Contributed to problem formulation and method design; implemented training and evaluation, reproduced strong baselines, and built fixed-K (2, 5, 20) and mixed-K (2–20) protocols. Conducted ablations, scale analyses, metric audits, visualization, and manuscript writing.

Evaluation

On HumanML3D, a single model covers K = 2–20 without sparsity-specific retraining, test-time candidate selection, or trajectory post-processing.

First authorSubmitted to AAAI 2027Feb – Jun 2026

PAIC: Position-Aligned Interval Conditioning for Text-Guided Human Motion In-Betweening

Zexuan Wang, Zeyu Ling, Xitian Huang, Yujie Hou, Yiwei Sheng

Makes each missing frame's local relationship to its neighboring keyframes explicit, combining interval conditioning with separate anchor and text memories.

Diffusion modelsText conditioningHuman motion
Method & contribution

Method

Encodes boundary distances, relative phase, interval length, and support validity. Combines a refinable coarse transition proposal with anchor and text memories in a single-stage masked diffusion model.

Contribution

Led problem formulation, implementation, model training, and writing. Established a unified evaluation protocol and ran comparisons, ablations, gap-length robustness tests, and fixed-keyframe semantic-response tests.

Evaluation

On HumanML3D, obtained lower FID and boundary error than task-matched base models across observation densities.

First authorManuscriptFeb – Sep 2025

Content-robust no-reference defocus estimation with asymmetric-attention learning and sigma calibration

Zexuan Wang, Xiaohua Xia

Estimates continuous image focus while reducing content-dependent bias, using asymmetric attention and sigma-based calibration.

Computer visionImage qualityContent robustness
Method & contribution

Method

Constructed multi-level Gaussian-defocus data from Waterloo images with continuous sigma-based labels. Combined asymmetric dual-branch attention, cross-scale fusion, and score calibration to separate defocus cues from content bias.

Contribution

Led the full study, including data generation, model design, training, cross-dataset evaluation, ablations, robustness and interpretability analyses, and manuscript preparation.
